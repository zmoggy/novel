IMAGES FOLDER
=============

PLAYER CHARACTER PORTRAIT
--------------------------
Place your player character portrait image in this folder as:
  player.png (or player.jpg)

This image will appear on the LEFT side of the dialogue box when talking to NPCs.

Image Requirements:
- Format: PNG (recommended) or JPG
- Recommended size: 300x400 pixels or larger
- The image will be displayed in the dialogue box portrait area (180x200px)
- Image should show your character from chest/waist up for best results

NPC CHARACTER PORTRAITS
------------------------
Place NPC character portraits in the /images/characters/ subfolder.
See /images/characters/README.txt for more details.

NPC portraits will appear on the RIGHT side of the dialogue box.
